---
title: Provisioning
description: Automate the user and group lifecycle from your customers' directories.
---

Provisioning keeps user and group data in sync between a customer's identity
provider and Qeet ID — so when someone joins, moves team or leaves, their access
follows automatically. It's part of the core product, not an enterprise upsell.

<Cards>
  <Card title="SCIM 2.0" href="/docs/provisioning/scim" description="Inbound Users & Groups sync from Okta, Entra and others." />
  <Card title="LDAP / Active Directory" href="/docs/provisioning/ldap" description="Authenticate and provision against a directory server." />
</Cards>

<Callout type="info">
  SSO and provisioning are complementary: [SAML SSO](/docs/authentication/enterprise-sso)
  handles _sign-in_, while SCIM/LDAP handle the _lifecycle_ of the accounts that
  sign in. Most enterprise deployments use both.
</Callout>
